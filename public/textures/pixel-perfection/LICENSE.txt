Pixel Perfection Community Edition - block textures
====================================================

Original resource pack "Pixel Perfection" created by Hugh "XSSheep" Rutland.
Community Edition updates by StonePendant (1.14), freejusticehere (1.15.2),
Stingraych, Nova_Wostra and lazerl0rd (1.16), maintained at:
https://github.com/Athemis/PixelPerfectionCE

License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0)
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

Changes made for Webmatica (atlas.png / atlas.json):
- Only the block textures (assets/minecraft/textures/block/*.png) are used.
  The pack's models, blockstates, sounds and other files are not included.
- Animated textures were reduced to their first frame.
- Random variants (e.g. oak_leaves1.png) and OptiFine emissive overlays
  (*_e.png) were left out.
- The remaining 16x16 tiles were repacked into a single PNG atlas.
